# Benchmarks

The known-answer tests LoadPath is pinned to, distilled from
[reports/Rigid block benchmark scenarios.md](../reports/Rigid%20block%20benchmark%20scenarios.md)
(IDs `B-`) and [reports/Structural failure case studies.md](../reports/Structural%20failure%20case%20studies.md)
(IDs `F-`). One row per test candidate. When a row becomes a test, its pinned number and source
get a row in [ANCHORS.md](ANCHORS.md) under the test's name. IDs are stable: never renumber,
retire a row by moving it to "Not adopted".

**Merge.** The two orders interleave by what each test isolates: contact geometry (failure
phase 1) first, then the exact tier (benchmark tier 1 with failure phase 2), friction switches
(benchmark tier 2), conventions (benchmark tier 3 with failure phase 3), invariance (failure
phase 4), experiments as brackets (benchmark experimental tier), support-removal topology
(failure phase 5), bonded joints and strength laws (benchmark bonded tier with the strength half
of failure phase 6), and last the dynamic half of failure phase 6, which only a host can run.

**Tags.** RB rigid blocks; NT no tension; NS no sliding; CF Coulomb friction; FS finite strength
(cohesion, tension or crushing enters); EXP experiment, answer is a band.

**Slice** (DESIGN §8). 1 model; 2 statics; 3 cascade; 4 collapse certificate; 5 catalogue and
fixtures; 6 scale; 7 referee LP; "—" no library slice (needs time integration, which the library
never does, DESIGN §2.3). A threshold on a statically determinate assembly runs at 2. A threshold
on an indeterminate assembly needs slice 4's upper bound for its fall side (its stand side runs
at 2). A fall direction or a named moving cluster needs 4; a release sequence needs 3.

**Markers.** "derived": arithmetic in the report or note from published inputs, not a figure the
source prints. "unverified": the report or note flags the number or input as not reproduced, not
retrieved, or second-hand.

## 4. Conventions: flow rule, arch formulation, strength path

The engine declares what it is before a number can be asserted, and the declaration goes in the
test name. LoadPath is non-associative (DESIGN §9 row 8): it lands between the MPEC and LP
columns; the referee must hit the LP column. The two strength rows here are the first that
convert a stress to a force, so the first that catch a 100× unit error. Exit: the engine lands
inside every published bracket and reports the sliding share of dissipation.

## Not adopted

Excluded by the reports themselves.

- **Housner's half-sine and rectangular-pulse overturning formulas**: wrong; the block overturns in free vibration, after the pulse ([Makris & Roussos](https://peer.berkeley.edu/publications/1998-05)).
- **D'Ayala (2003) closed forms for the Pavia façades** (0.438–0.545): 40–67 % high, no torsional friction on the crack line ([Bui et al. 2017](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/144603/3/BUI-ALI-VASILIS-MANUSCRIP-REVISED-V17new_Final.pdf)).
- **Corbelled domes**: ring compression lets them stand where 2-D corbelling says they fall ([Rovero & Tonietti 2014](https://flore.unifi.it/handle/2158/820494)).
- **Bargower and other deep-fill bridges** (5600 kN, 8 % from the barrel) ([Sarhosis et al.](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/146294/2/SARHOS~1.pdf)).
- **UPC BA1/BA2 collapse loads** (60 and 95 kN against ring-alone 15 and 16.1 kN): spandrels and ties carry the difference; hinge order kept as B-ARC-12.
- **Flemish-bond diagonal compression** (115–179 kN): needs brick fracture ([Segura et al.](https://upcommons.upc.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/77631472-a1eb-4bc2-b2ad-5bfcb4bd5f02/content)).
- **Propped cantilever**: its reactions need joint compliance a rigid-block solver does not have.
- **Couplet's 1730 answer** (t/r = 0.101, hinges at 45°): history, not a target.
- **LNEC h/b = 8 block** (measured 0.154 rad against 0.119): not for calibration.
- **3DEC masonry demonstrations** (arch bridge creeping at 6000 kN, Flemish wall, flying buttress): joint stiffness, no comparator ([3DEC Highway Loading](https://docs.itascacg.com/itasca950/3dec/docproject/source/examples/HighwayLoading.html)).
- **COMPAS CRA examples** (20-block arch, span 10, height 5, thickness 0.5, μ = 0.7; three unit cubes rotated 20°) and **Gilbert, Casapulla & Ahmed 2006**: inputs public, answers paywalled ([compas_cra](https://github.com/BlockResearchGroup/compas_cra/tree/main/docs/examples); [Gilbert et al.](https://iris.unina.it/handle/11588/300870)).
- **FACE 2014-02 block wall**: height, bracing and gust only in the unretrieved report.
- **Medellín confined wall**: an RC-framed system.
- **Colas, Morel & Garnier and Bath EPSRC retaining walls**: fill heights paywalled ([Coventry](https://pureportal.coventry.ac.uk/en/publications/full-scale-field-trials-to-assess-dry-stone-retaining-wall-stabil/); [Bath](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/drystone-retaining-walls-from-full-scale-testing-to-construction-)).
- **L'Aquila walls as numeric cases**: no source gives one wall's height, thickness, restraint and PGA together; the tie flips are kept in F-GAB-01.
- **Any Pavia, Noto or St Mark's variant that expects a fall**.

Held back by this distillation, with the reason.

- **Textbook frame cases** (three-hinged portal thrust PL/4h, overhanging beam tipping past its back span): the report cites no source, and every anchor needs one (DESIGN §10, 2026-10-10).
- **Chimney rupture height** (one third to one half of H, [Varieschi & Kamiya](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0210033)): needs a fracture criterion; pieces never fail internally here (DESIGN §1).
- **Liberton tip displacement** (130–140 mm at the top) and **Southampton's crest deflection** (≤ 1 % of fill height before FoS ≈ 1.05, [eprint](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/39421)): displacements, never a break assertion (DESIGN §6). Corroboration only.
- **Explicit-stepper Δt ≤ 0.5·Δt_crit** and **Voronoi bar uniform strain within 1 % at ν = 0** ([FraMCoS-4](https://framcos.org/FraMCoS-4/773.pdf)): the library has no time stepper and meshes nothing.
