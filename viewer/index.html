LoadPath E2E tests

Every entry is recorded by a test while it runs, so this page always shows the suite as it is. Each shows the structure before the run and the engine's state after the cascade; when the test expects something else, a button switches the after view to the expected outcome. The mark on each entry says whether its test passed, failed or is deferred to later work. Released bodies move along the collapse mechanism the certificate computed, coloured one colour per falling piece; removed bodies are dashed ghosts; severed joints are red.

The load factor is the certificate's multiplier on every applied load at once, gravity included: at or above 1 the structure stands, below 1 it collapses. On dry joints it reads either about 0 or the cap of 1000, since friction alone has no strength scale.
